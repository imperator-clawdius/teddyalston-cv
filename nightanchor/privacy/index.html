NightAnchor Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026

NightAnchor is designed to keep sleep enforcement data on your device. The app does not use an account system, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, crash-reporting SDK, backend service, or third-party tracking.

Data Collection

NightAnchor does not collect data from you or your device for the developer. The app's App Store privacy answer is Data Not Collected.

On-Device Data

NightAnchor stores the following data locally on your device so the app and its Screen Time extensions can enforce your selected sleep policy:

  • sleep window settings
  • selected apps, categories, and websites represented by Apple's opaque Screen Time tokens
  • saved sleep-place coordinates and radius
  • sleep-place dwell state
  • safeword hash
  • local enforcement and override action log

This data stays on device in the app's container or shared app group. It is not uploaded to a server by NightAnchor.

Selfie Verification

Selfie images are processed on device for Vision face detection. NightAnchor does not store selfie photos and does not upload them.

Location

NightAnchor uses location only to save and verify your sleep place and to support the one-hour location dwell release path. Location data is stored locally for enforcement state and is not sold, shared, or uploaded by NightAnchor.

Safeword

The safeword is stored locally as a salted, iterated hash. NightAnchor does not store the plain-text safeword after saving it.
