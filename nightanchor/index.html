Native iPhone app

NightAnchor support

NightAnchor is a native iOS sleep enforcement app for people who keep working past their planned stop time.

The app uses Apple Screen Time APIs to shield selected apps, app categories, and websites during a user-defined sleep window. It can release those shields after a selfie check-in at the saved sleep place, after one continuous hour inside the saved sleep-place geofence, or through a safeword override.
